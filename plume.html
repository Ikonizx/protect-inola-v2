These numbers are transcribed from the graphic, not independently verified against the tables here. DEQ lists the corrected September 3 application as under technical review. Official application and supporting documents.

No zone resizing is justified by these dimensions alone. Stack-specific emission rates, exhaust temperature and velocity, stack diameters and coordinates, building downwash, terrain, meteorology, and modeled concentrations are needed. The current bands remain illustrative and are not calculated from these dimensions or the 0.64 lb/t proposal.
